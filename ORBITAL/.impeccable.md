## Design Context

### Users
A flight surgeon on Earth reviewing one astronaut's sleep and light record, checking the model's recommended
schedule, and deciding what to send to the crew. Hackathon judges see the same screen in the demo.

### Brand Personality
Calm, clinical, trustworthy. The screen supports a medical decision: evidence next to the recommendation, no hype.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing ORBITAL look (dark navy surfaces, mint accent for the recommendation, amber for current light,
violet for sleep). Dark theme fits mission-control review. Re-layout only; the team designer owns the visual style.

### Costume & Theme
Look source: redesign, keep the soul (existing tokens and fonts). Theme: dark, existing. Chosen 2026-11-15.

### Design Principles
1. Decision first: the recommendation and the evidence for it sit at the top, side by side.
2. Raw data below: last-night metrics, then the replay and manual plan controls.
3. One primary action per screen (preview the recommendation); everything else is secondary.
4. Say what is estimated vs measured, close to the number.
